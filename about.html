About Ezzzzz

Ezzzzz is a student project built as an online store for original digital artwork. Our small team designed and coded every page from scratch as part of the HTML & CSS Basics assignment.

Our Mission

  • Give independent digital artists a simple storefront
  • Keep the browsing experience clean and fast
  • Practice real HTML and CSS fundamentals as a team

We started this project as a class assignment, but we try to treat it like a real store: clear categories, honest pricing, and a page that still works if you open it on your phone.
